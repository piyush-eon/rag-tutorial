# Remote Work Policy

**Owner:** People Team · **Version:** 2.4 · **Last updated:** 8 January 2026

## 1. Overview
Northwind runs a hybrid model. We believe teams work best when they meet in person regularly and have focus time at home.

## 2. Office days
Employees come to the office **3 days a week**: Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday by default. Teams may agree a different set of days with their manager.

## 3. Working from home
Monday and Friday are remote days. You must be reachable on chat between 10am and 4pm local time.

## 4. Working from another location
You may work from another city or country for up to 20 days a year with your manager's approval. Tax and legal rules apply, so tell the People team before you travel.

## 5. Equipment
Northwind provides a laptop and a monitor. A home-office allowance of $300 a year can be claimed through the Expenses app.

## 6. Security
Use the VPN when working outside the office. Never work on company data on public Wi-Fi without it.
