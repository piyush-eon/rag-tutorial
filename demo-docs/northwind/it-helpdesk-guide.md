# IT Helpdesk Guide

**Owner:** IT · **Version:** 5.1 · **Last updated:** 5 March 2026

## 1. Getting help
Open a ticket at it.northwind.test or email helpdesk@northwind.test. Urgent issues: ext. 4000, 8am to 8pm on weekdays.

## 2. VPN problems
The company VPN is needed to reach internal tools from outside the office.

| Error shown | What to do |
|---|---|
| **ERR_4012** | Your VPN token has expired. Reset your token in the IT portal, then reconnect. |
| ERR_2207 | The client is out of date. Install the latest version from the Software Center. |
| ERR_5101 | Your device clock is wrong. Turn on automatic date and time. |

If the error is not listed, restart the VPN client and try again before opening a ticket.

## 3. Passwords
Passwords expire every 180 days. Reset yours at id.northwind.test. After 5 failed attempts the account locks for 15 minutes.

| Error shown | What to do |
|---|---|
| ERR_4201 | Your account is locked. Wait 15 minutes, or call ext. 4000 to unlock it now. |
| ERR_2410 | Your new password was used before. Choose one you haven't used in the last 10 changes. |

## 4. Laptops
- New laptops are issued on your first day.
- Replacement for a broken laptop usually takes 2 working days.
- Report a lost or stolen laptop to IT immediately, day or night.

| Error shown | What to do |
|---|---|
| ERR_4120 | The docking station isn't recognised. Unplug it for 10 seconds, then plug it back in. |
| ERR_1204 | The battery needs replacing. Open a ticket and IT will swap it the same day. |

## 5. Software
Install approved tools from the Software Center. For anything else, request approval through a ticket.

| Error shown | What to do |
|---|---|
| ERR_4210 | The install was blocked because the tool isn't approved. Request approval through a ticket. |
| ERR_2014 | There isn't enough disk space. Free up at least 5 GB and try again. |

## 6. Printing and meeting-room screens
Printers are on every floor. Print jobs wait in a secure queue until you tap your badge on the printer. Meeting-room screens connect with the Northwind-Guest-Cast network.

| Error shown | What to do |
|---|---|
| ERR_4021 | Your print job was held because your badge isn't registered for printing. Register it at the reception desk. |
| ERR_3307 | The printer is out of toner. Pick another printer on the same floor and report it in a ticket. |

## 7. Wi-Fi
Use the Northwind-Staff network in all offices. Guests use Northwind-Guest, which has no access to internal tools.

| Error shown | What to do |
|---|---|
| ERR_4102 | Your Wi-Fi certificate has expired. Re-enrol your laptop from the Software Center, then reconnect. |
| ERR_1650 | Too many devices on your account. Remove an old device at id.northwind.test. |
