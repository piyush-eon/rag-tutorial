# Leave Policy

**Owner:** People Team · **Version:** 3.0 · **Last updated:** 2 January 2026

## 1. Annual leave
Full-time employees get 25 days of paid annual leave a year, plus public holidays. Leave builds up monthly. New joiners get a pro-rated amount.

## 2. Booking leave
Request leave in the People app. Your manager replies within 3 working days. Requests of more than 10 days in a row should be made at least 6 weeks ahead.

## 3. Carry over
Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year. They must be used by 31 March.

## 4. Sick leave
- Up to 10 paid sick days a year.
- Tell your manager before your start time on the first day.
- A medical note is needed after 3 days in a row.

## 5. Parental leave
- Primary caregivers: 20 weeks at full pay.
- Secondary caregivers: 6 weeks at full pay.
- Leave can be taken any time in the first 12 months.

## 6. Other leave
Bereavement leave is 5 days for a close family member. Jury duty and civic duties are paid.
