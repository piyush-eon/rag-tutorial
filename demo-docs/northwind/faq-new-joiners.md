# New Joiner FAQ

**Owner:** People Team · **Version:** 1.5 · **Last updated:** 11 February 2026

**Q: What time do I start on my first day?**
10am, at reception. Your buddy will meet you.

**Q: When do I get paid?**
On the last working day of each month.

**Q: Where do I find my payslip?**
In the People app under "Pay".

**Q: How do I get a new laptop?**
IT gives you one on day one. If something is wrong, open a ticket at it.northwind.test.

**Q: What should I wear?**
Whatever you are comfortable in. Smart casual is typical when you meet customers.

**Q: Can I bring a guest to the office?**
Yes. Register them at reception a day ahead.

**Q: Who is my buddy?**
A colleague assigned to help you in the first 3 months. You will get their name before day one.

**Q: How do I join a team chat channel?**
Search for the channel in chat and click Join. Start with #general, #announcements and your team channel.

**Q: I have a question that is not here.**
Ask your buddy or email people@northwind.test.
