# Security Policy

**Owner:** Security · **Version:** 4.2 · **Last updated:** 18 February 2026

## 1. Why this matters
Customer data and company data are our most valuable assets. Everyone is responsible for protecting them.

## 2. Accounts
- Use a password manager. Never reuse a password.
- Turn on multi-factor authentication for every work account.
- Never share your login, even with IT.

## 3. Devices
- Lock your screen when you leave your desk.
- Keep disk encryption on.
- Install updates within 7 days of release.

## 4. Data handling
Data is classed as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Confidential and Restricted data must never be copied to personal devices or personal cloud storage.

## 5. Email and phishing
If a message looks suspicious, do not click it. Use the Report Phishing button, or forward it to security@northwind.test.

## 6. Reporting incidents
Report anything unusual, such as a lost device, a wrong email recipient or a suspicious login, within 1 hour to security@northwind.test or ext. 4999.

## 7. Consequences
Breaking this policy can lead to disciplinary action.
