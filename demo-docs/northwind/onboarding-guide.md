# Onboarding Guide

**Owner:** People Team · **Version:** 1.8 · **Last updated:** 15 February 2026

Welcome to Northwind. This guide covers your first week.

## Before day one
- Sign your contract in the People portal.
- Send a photo for your ID badge.
- Your laptop is shipped or ready at reception.

## Day one
1. Meet your manager and your onboarding buddy.
2. Collect your laptop and badge from IT at 10am.
3. Set up your email, chat and password manager.
4. Join the new-joiner welcome call at 3pm.

## Week one checklist
- [ ] Complete security training
- [ ] Read the Code of Conduct and the Security Policy
- [ ] Set up the VPN
- [ ] Meet each person on your team
- [ ] Agree 30-60-90 day goals with your manager

## Tools you will use
| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Chat | Day-to-day messages |
| Wiki | Team documents |
| Expenses app | Claims |
| People app | Leave and payslips |

## Who to ask
Your buddy for everyday questions, the People team for contracts and benefits, IT for equipment.
