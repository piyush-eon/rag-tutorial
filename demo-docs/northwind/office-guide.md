# Office Guide

**Owner:** Workplace · **Version:** 1.4 · **Last updated:** 9 February 2026

## Address
Northwind HQ, 22 Harbour Street, Level 4. The nearest station is Harbour Central, 4 minutes' walk.

## Opening hours
The office is open 7:30am to 8pm, Monday to Friday. Badge access only outside these hours.

## Wi-Fi
- **Northwind-Staff:** sign in with your work account.
- **Northwind-Guest:** for visitors, password changes every Monday at reception.

## Parking and bikes
There are 20 car spaces, allocated by lottery each month. Bike storage and showers are in the basement and need your badge.

## Meeting rooms
Book rooms in the calendar app. Rooms are released automatically if nobody checks in within 10 minutes.

## Visitors
Register visitors at reception at least a day in advance. Visitors must wear a badge and be escorted.

## Food
The kitchen has free tea, coffee and fruit. Lunch is not provided, but there is a microwave on every floor.

## Problems
Message #workplace-help or email workplace@northwind.test.
