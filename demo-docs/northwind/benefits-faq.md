# Benefits FAQ

**Owner:** People Team · **Version:** 2.0 · **Last updated:** 10 February 2026

**Q: What health cover do I get?**
Medical, dental and vision cover for you and your dependants, starting on your first day. Northwind pays 90% of the premium.

**Q: Is there a learning budget?**
Yes. Every employee gets $1,200 a year for courses, books and conferences. Submit the request in the People app before you buy.

**Q: Do you match retirement savings?**
We match your contribution up to 5% of your salary.

**Q: Is there a wellness allowance?**
$40 a month for gym or fitness, claimed through the Expenses app.

**Q: What about mental health support?**
A free, confidential counselling line is available 24 hours a day. The number is on the intranet.

**Q: Can I take unpaid leave?**
Up to 4 weeks, with your manager's and the People team's approval.

**Q: When do benefits start?**
Health cover starts on day one. Retirement matching starts after 3 months.
