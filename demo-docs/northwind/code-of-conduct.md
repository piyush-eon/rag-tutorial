# Code of Conduct

**Owner:** People Team · **Version:** 2.2 · **Last updated:** 4 January 2026

## 1. Our promise
Northwind is a place where people can do their best work and feel respected.

## 2. What we expect
- Treat everyone with respect, in person and online.
- Be honest and keep your word.
- Share credit and give feedback kindly.
- Respect other people's time and focus.

## 3. What we do not accept
Harassment, discrimination, bullying, threats and retaliation of any kind. This covers office events, work trips and online channels.

## 4. Conflicts of interest
Tell your manager if you have a personal connection to a supplier, customer or candidate.

## 5. Gifts
Do not accept gifts worth more than $50 from suppliers or customers.

## 6. Speaking up
Report concerns to your manager, the People team or the anonymous hotline at conduct.northwind.test. We do not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.

## 7. What happens next
Every report is reviewed within 5 working days. Outcomes can range from coaching to dismissal.
