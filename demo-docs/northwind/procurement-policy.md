# Procurement Policy

**Owner:** Finance · **Version:** 1.6 · **Last updated:** 14 January 2026

## 1. Purpose
This policy covers buying software, hardware and services from outside suppliers.

## 2. Before you buy
- Check the Software Center and the approved vendor list first.
- Compare at least 2 quotes for any purchase above $2,500.
- Do not sign up for a paid tool with a personal or company card without approval.

## 3. Approval
Follow the Approval Matrix. Purchases above $5,000 also need a short business case.

## 4. Software and data
Any software that stores company or customer data needs a security review. Request it at least 15 working days before you need to go live.

## 5. Hardware
IT orders all laptops, monitors and phones. Do not buy hardware yourself.

## 6. Vendors
New vendors must complete the vendor form, share proof of insurance and pass a basic risk check. Payment terms are normally 30 days from invoice.

## 7. Renewals
Owners of subscriptions are asked 60 days before renewal whether to continue. Unused licences are cancelled.
