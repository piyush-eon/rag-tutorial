# Expense Policy

**Owner:** Finance · **Version:** 4.0 · **Last updated:** 3 February 2026

## 1. What can be claimed
Costs that you paid personally while doing your job: travel, meals while travelling, client entertainment, small equipment and approved training.

## 2. How to claim
1. Take a photo of every receipt.
2. Create a claim in the Expenses app and attach the receipts.
3. Submit the claim for your manager's approval.

**Submit receipts within 30 days** of the expense. Claims older than 30 days need a written exception from Finance.

## 3. Meals
Meals are reimbursed up to **$60 per day** while travelling. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## 4. Client entertainment
Up to $90 per person per event. Name every attendee and the business reason on the claim.

## 5. Not reimbursable
- Personal items, fines and parking tickets
- Upgrades to a higher travel class
- Gifts above $50 per person

## 6. Payment
Approved claims are paid in the next monthly payroll run, usually within 10 working days.
