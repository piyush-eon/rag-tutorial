# Holiday Calendar 2026

**Owner:** People Team · **Version:** 1.0 · **Last updated:** 2 December 2025

Offices are closed on these days. All dates are paid company holidays.

| Date | Day | Holiday |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Jan | Thursday | New Year's Day |
| 3 Apr | Friday | Good Friday |
| 6 Apr | Monday | Easter Monday |
| 4 May | Monday | Spring Bank Holiday |
| 25 May | Monday | Late May Bank Holiday |
| 31 Aug | Monday | Summer Bank Holiday |
| 24 Dec | Thursday | Christmas Eve (half day) |
| 25 Dec | Friday | Christmas Day |
| 28 Dec | Monday | Boxing Day (observed) |

## Company shutdown
The offices also close from 29 December to 31 December. These three days do not come out of your annual leave.

## Working on a holiday
If you must work on a company holiday, you get a day off in lieu, to be taken within 60 days.
