# Travel Policy

**Owner:** Finance · **Version:** 3.2 · **Last updated:** 12 January 2026

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains how Northwind employees book and pay for business travel. It applies to all full-time staff and contractors.

## 2. Booking
- Book through the Northwind travel portal at least 14 days before departure where possible.
- Employees should book **budget airfare** (economy class) for all trips. Business class is not reimbursable.
- Choose the lowest fare within a 2-hour window of your preferred departure time.
- Rail is preferred over air for trips under 400 km.

## 3. Hotels
- Book a standard single room through the portal. Nightly cap: $180 in major cities, $130 elsewhere.
- Stay at partner hotels where available. They offer corporate rates and direct billing.

## 4. Daily allowance
Meals and local transport while travelling are covered by the per diem in the Expense Policy. Do not claim both the per diem and receipts for the same meal.

## 5. Changes and cancellations
Cancel unused bookings in the portal within 24 hours. Unused non-refundable tickets must be reported to Finance so the credit can be reused.

## 6. Approvals
Trips above the limits in the Approval Matrix need sign-off before you book.

## 7. Questions
Email travel@northwind.test or post in #travel-help.
