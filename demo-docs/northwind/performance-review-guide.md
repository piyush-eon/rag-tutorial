# Performance Review Guide

**Owner:** People Team · **Version:** 2.0 · **Last updated:** 6 January 2026

## 1. The cycle
Reviews happen twice a year: a mid-year check-in in June and a full review in December.

## 2. Steps
1. **Self review:** write what you achieved against your goals (open for 2 weeks).
2. **Peer feedback:** pick 3 colleagues to give feedback.
3. **Manager review:** your manager writes an assessment and proposes a rating.
4. **Calibration:** managers compare ratings across teams for fairness.
5. **Conversation:** you meet your manager to discuss the result.

## 3. Ratings

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Outstanding |
| 4 | Exceeds expectations |
| 3 | Meets expectations |
| 2 | Needs improvement |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory |

## 4. Promotions
Promotion decisions are made once a year with the December review. Managers submit a case based on sustained impact at the next level.

## 5. Pay
Pay changes take effect on 1 February.

## 6. Support
If your rating surprises you, talk to your manager first, then to the People team.
