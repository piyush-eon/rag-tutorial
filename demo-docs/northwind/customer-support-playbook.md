# Customer Support Playbook

**Owner:** Support · **Version:** 3.1 · **Last updated:** 16 February 2026

## 1. Principles
Reply quickly, be clear and own the problem until it is solved.

## 2. Priorities and response times

| Priority | Example | First reply | Target fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Urgent | Customer cannot use the product | 1 hour | 4 hours |
| High | Key feature broken | 4 hours | 1 working day |
| Normal | Question or small bug | 1 working day | 3 working days |
| Low | Feature request | 2 working days | None |

## 3. Handling a ticket
1. Read the full history before replying.
2. Reply in plain language and confirm what you understood.
3. If you need engineering, create an engineering ticket and link it.
4. Tell the customer what will happen next and when.

## 4. Refunds
- Agents can refund up to $200 without approval.
- Refunds from $201 to $1,000 need the Support Lead.
- Anything above $1,000 needs the Head of Support.
- Refunds are possible within 30 days of purchase.

## 5. Escalation
Escalate angry customers, legal threats and security concerns to the Support Lead straight away.

## 6. Closing
Close a ticket only after the customer confirms the fix, or after 7 days without a reply.
