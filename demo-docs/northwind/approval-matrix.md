# Approval Matrix

**Owner:** Finance · **Version:** 2.1 · **Last updated:** 20 January 2026

Spending needs approval before you commit the money. The approver depends on the amount.

## 1. Purchases and spending

| Amount | Who approves |
|---|---|
| Up to $500 | Your direct manager |
| $501 to $5,000 | Your department head |
| $5,001 to $25,000 | Department head and Finance Director |
| Over $25,000 | Chief Financial Officer |

## 2. Trips
All travel over $2,000 must be approved by your **department head** via the Finance portal. Submit the request at least 10 working days before you plan to book. Trips under $2,000 only need your direct manager's approval.

## 3. Hiring and headcount
New roles need approval from the department head, the Finance Director and the People team.

## 4. Contracts
Any contract with an outside company is reviewed by Legal first. Signing authority sits with the department head up to $25,000.

## 5. Emergencies
If you cannot reach an approver, contact the on-call Finance manager on ext. 4100 and record the reason afterwards.
