# Travel Policy

**Owner:** Operations · **Version:** 2.0 · **Last updated:** 9 January 2026

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets the rules for business travel at Globex.

## 2. Air travel
Globex employees may fly **business class** on flights over 6 hours. All other flights are economy class.

## 3. Booking
Book through the Globex travel desk at least 7 days ahead. Fares are paid by the company card issued to travellers.

## 4. Hotels
Up to $220 per night in major cities, $150 elsewhere.

## 5. Approvals
Trips above $3,000 need approval from your regional director.

## 6. Questions
Email travel@globex.test.
