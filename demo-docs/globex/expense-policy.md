# Expense Policy

**Owner:** Finance · **Version:** 1.9 · **Last updated:** 12 January 2026

## 1. Claims
Submit claims in the Globex Spend app with a photo of each receipt.

## 2. Deadline
Submit receipts within **14 days** of the expense. Late claims are paid only with the approval of Finance.

## 3. Meals
Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to **$45 per day**.

## 4. Client entertainment
Up to $75 per person.

## 5. Not reimbursable
Alcohol, personal items and fines.

## 6. Payment
Approved claims are paid every two weeks.

## 7. Questions
Email finance@globex.test.
