# Remote Work Policy

**Owner:** People Operations · **Version:** 1.3 · **Last updated:** 5 January 2026

## 1. Overview
Globex is a **fully remote** company. We have no required office days and no headquarters.

## 2. Working hours
Agree your hours with your team. Everyone must overlap for at least 4 hours a day with their team.

## 3. Equipment
Globex provides a laptop. A one-time home-office setup budget of $800 is available, and a monthly internet stipend of $50.

## 4. Meetups
Teams meet in person twice a year. Travel and hotel are paid by Globex.

## 5. Working abroad
You can work from another country for up to 30 days a year. Tell People Operations in advance.

## 6. Questions
Email people@globex.test.
